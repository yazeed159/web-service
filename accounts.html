Trading accounts

Real accounts

Each real account keeps its own trades, deposits and equity curve. The switcher in the top bar (or View below) decides what every page shows — one account, a few, or all of them combined.

Money in & out, and periods

A period starts whenever you add money after a break (or any time you choose) and runs until the next one starts. Each period can be viewed on its own across the whole site, and has a place for what you reflected on.

Paper trading attempts

Every reset on the Practice tab is saved as its own attempt, so you can compare experiments. Paper results never touch your real accounts’ numbers.

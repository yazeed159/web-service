Trading accounts

Real accounts

Real-money broker accounts only. Each real account keeps its own trades, deposits and equity curve. The switcher in the top bar (or View below) decides what every page shows — one account, a few, or all of them combined.

Paper accounts (from your broker)

Your broker’s paper-trading account, tracked on its own. It’s left out of “All real accounts” everywhere — pick it in the top-bar switcher (or View below) when you want to look at it. This is separate from the Practice simulator on this site.

Money in & out, and periods

A period starts whenever you add money after a break (or any time you choose) and runs until the next one starts. Each period can be viewed on its own across the whole site, and has a place for what you reflected on.

Scan & Session
Entry

Opening range breakout: buys the first bar above the high of the first N minutes. Red-candle break: buys the first bar that breaks above the high of the last down candle. Donchian: a rolling ORB — buys the break of the highest high in the last N bars. Inside-bar break: buys the break of a one-bar consolidation's high. VWAP reclaim: buys the first bar back above session VWAP after price dropped below it. 9 EMA dip & reclaim: buys the break of the last candle that dipped to/through the EMA — the classic "9EMA dip" gapper trade. EMA reclaim: buys the first bar back above the EMA after price closed below it. MACD bullish cross: buys the close of the bar where MACD crosses above its signal line. RSI oversold bounce: buys the close of the first bar RSI climbs back above the oversold level.

Position Building (scale in / trail-protect)

Off by default. When on, the position opens at a fraction of full size and adds more as the trade proves itself -- price makes a new high, holds there for a bit (no new high, but not stopped out), then breaks above that held high again. Never adds to a trade that isn't already working.

No fixed profit targets or partial exits -- the whole position exits together, whenever it exits. Instead, once enabled, the stop ratchets up (never down) to lock in a growing share of the peak gain the further the trade runs -- protects more of the move the deeper it works, rather than one fixed giveback the whole time.

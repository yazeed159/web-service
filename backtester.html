Entry

Opening range breakout: buys the first bar above the high of the first N minutes. Red-candle break: buys the first bar that breaks above the high of the last down candle. Donchian: a rolling ORB — buys the break of the highest high in the last N bars. Inside-bar break: buys the break of a one-bar consolidation's high. VWAP reclaim: buys the first bar back above session VWAP after price dropped below it.

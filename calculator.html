What this is for Before you enter a trade, this tells you how many shares to buy so that if you're wrong and hit your stop, you only lose a small, planned slice of your account — not a random amount that happens to be however many shares felt right. That's also why your balance below moves with your P&L: risking "1% of your account" only makes sense against your real, current balance, so it automatically scales down after losses and up after wins instead of staying frozen at whatever you started with.
